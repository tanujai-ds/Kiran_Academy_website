Questions students ask

  

    
Do I need a coding background?
No. Courses start from the basics. A counsellor will place you in a beginner or fast-track batch after a short call about your education and goals.

    
Is placement support included?
Yes. Interview preparation and access to campus drives are part of every course. Support continues after the batch until you accept an offer, subject to attendance and assignment completion.

    
Can I attend online?
Yes. Online and classroom batches run for most courses. You get the same projects, mocks and placement pipeline either way.

    
How do I try a class first?
Fill in the demo form. We call you, share batch timings, and schedule a free class so you can meet the trainer before you enrol.

    
What is the typical fee and EMI?
Fees vary by course and batch. Counsellors share the current fee, EMI options and any campus-drive scholarships on the demo call. Dummy figures are not listed here on purpose.

    
Which branch should I pick?
Karve Nagar, Hadapsar and Chinchwad serve Pune; Nagpur has its own campus. If you travel or work shifts, start online and switch to classroom for project week.
